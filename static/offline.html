If a storm comes

  • Lightning: if you hear thunder, go inside a solid building or a closed car.
  • Flooding: never walk or drive through flood water; move to higher ground.
  • Roads: do not cross flooded roads, low bridges or causeways.
  • Rivers: stay away from rivers and streams; they can rise suddenly.
  • Livestock: move animals early; do not go out once lightning starts.

If a storm comes

  • Lightning: if you hear thunder, go inside a solid building or a closed car.
  • Flooding: never walk or drive through flood water; move to higher ground.
  • Roads: do not cross flooded roads, low bridges or causeways.
  • Rivers: stay away from rivers and streams; they can rise suddenly.
  • Livestock: move animals early; do not go out once lightning starts.

Where this comes from

While connected, the system retrieves ECMWF weather forecasts (via Open-Meteo), and a Random Forest model trained on historical ERA5 data estimates the storm risk for Matatiele for the next hour. A score at or above 35% is a warning decision. This page keeps a copy of that result, saved on this device.

What works offline

You can read the saved risk score, warning decision, model outlook, alerts, safety advice and emergency numbers. New weather forecasts, new model results and new warnings need a connection: this page cannot receive new warnings without a connection. Save again from the dashboard when you have signal.
